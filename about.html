About Haixing Fabrics

Professional Woven Fabric Manufacturer & Global Exporter from China

Haixing Fabrics is the international brand of a large-scale woven fabric manufacturer based in Guangdong, China, supported by a dedicated sales and export office. From yarn to fabric, from China to global markets.

Manufacturing Entity

佛山市南海区海娃纺织有限公司

Our production base, located in Nanhai District, Foshan, Guangdong Province — operating 500+ imported Belgian Picanol weaving machines across an integrated woven fabric production line.

Sales Showroom

佛山市顺德区金棉洋纺织品有限公司

Our sales showroom, located in Shunde District, Foshan, Guangdong Province — handling international enquiries, sampling, quotation and export logistics under the Haixing Fabrics brand.
